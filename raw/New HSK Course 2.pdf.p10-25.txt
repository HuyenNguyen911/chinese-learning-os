Instructions for Use
New HSK Course 2 is designed for learners who have completed approximately 30-36 class hours,
mastered around 300 vocabulary words, and are preparing to take the HSK (Level 2) exam.
Basic Design
Based on the new HSK syllabus and the design philosophy and principles of this textbook series, New
HSK Course 2 consists of 15 lessons, includes vocabulary aligned with the 200 words required by the new
syllabus with slight extensions, and covers 45 language points. The recommended total teaching time is
3045 class hours, with 2-3 class hours suggested for each lesson.
New HSK Course 2 continues to follow a learner-centered philosophy and adopts a goal-oriented,
needs-based approach, with the principle of cultivating learners’ Chinese communicative competence. The
content and language points of the lessons are consistent with the new HSK syllabus, including tasks, topics,
vocabulary, grammar, and characters. In actual teaching and learning, it is recommended to progress in an
orderly manner according to the structure of the textbook.
Lesson Sections
The lesson sections of this book include Objectives, Warm-Ups, Texts, Xiaoyu’s Classroom,
Comprehensive Exercises, Classroom Activity, Xiaoyu’s Bonus Content, and Learning Summary.
Objectives: Key points of teaching and learning in each lesson.
Warm-Up: A two-part activity that includes a word-picture matching section to assess learners’
preparation of vocabulary, and a second section featuring word-picture matching, fill-in-the-blank, and table
completion for introducing or practicing language points.
Texts: Four texts, with the first three being situational dialogues and the fourth being a narrative text.
Xiaoyu’s Classroom: Explanations and practice of language points, appearing after each text with
exercises following example sentences for practice.
Comprehensive Exercises: Integrated exercises for vocabulary and language points of the current
lesson, arranged flexibly for in-class or after-class use.
Classroom Activity: Role-play, pair work, and group survey are designed to help students integrate
what they have learned and complete communicative tasks.
Xiaoyu’s Bonus Content: Nine such sections providing essential cultural and informational points
related to each lesson.
Learning Summary: A review and reflection section appearing every three lessons for evaluation of
teaching and learning outcomes.
In addition, this book features “Xiaoyu’s Tip”, brief notes placed near relevant content to help learners
quickly grasp related knowledge.
The book comes with a workbook and a digital edition, accessible via the FLTRP Global Smart
Education Platform (https://global.unipus.cn). This platform offers audio, video, interactive modules, and
extra exercises. To activate your digital edition, please scan the QR code located on the back cover.
Additional teaching and learning resources can be viewed and downloaded on FLTRP CLT Resources (http://
www.fltrp-clt.com).
